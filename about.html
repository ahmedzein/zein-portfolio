About Me

I’m a Product Designer with 6+ years of experience designing digital products and experiences across SaaS platforms, government systems and marketing-led digital experiences.

My approach is end-to-end. From understanding the problem and researching user needs to shaping flows, designing interfaces, building design systems and creating responsive experiences, I enjoy being involved throughout the process.

I care about more than how a product looks. I think about how it works, how people experience it and how design can support meaningful business outcomes. Accessibility, usability and clarity are at the heart of my work, and I’m always looking for ways to make complex experiences feel simple and intuitive.

I’m currently based in London and open to opportunities where I can work alongside a collaborative product team, contribute to meaningful products and continue growing as a designer.

Inspiration beyond the screen

Great ideas don’t always come from sitting behind a screen.

Travelling has become one of the biggest influences on how I see and approach design. Experiencing different countries, cultures, environments and ways of life constantly gives me new perspectives.

I enjoy getting lost in unfamiliar places, discovering how people live and noticing the small details that make each place unique. From architecture and typography to food, transport and everyday interactions, there is always something to observe.

That curiosity carries into my work. I believe the more perspectives you experience, the more thoughtfully you can design for people who see the world differently from you.

For me, travelling isn’t just about seeing new places. It’s about staying curious, challenging what I already know and bringing those experiences back into my creative process.

Beyond design

Design is a big part of who I am, but it isn’t everything that defines me.

Outside of work, you’ll usually find me exploring something new, experimenting in the kitchen or finding ways to give back to communities that need support.

Cooking gives me a completely different creative outlet. There’s something rewarding about starting with a handful of ingredients, experimenting with different ideas and turning them into something people can enjoy. It’s a reminder that creativity doesn’t always need a screen.

Giving back is equally important to me. Through charity work, I’ve had the opportunity to contribute to projects that support people and communities in need. Whether that means helping provide food, supporting essential resources or contributing to a water project, I believe design and creativity are most meaningful when they can have a positive impact beyond ourselves.

These experiences shape the way I approach design: stay curious, keep learning, care about the details and always think about the people on the other side of what you create.
